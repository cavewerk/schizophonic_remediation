# Mosfets

## N-channel vs P-channel
N-channel, connect one terminal of solenoid to Voltage source - one to ground (center pin) on mosfet
P-channel, connect one terminal to ground (on power source). - one to voltage (center) on mosfet

## Gate source voltage (Vgs)
Max voltage tolerated at gate-source
- watch out for polarity

## Gate source threshold voltage (Vgs(th))
minimum voltage to turn on
min - typ. - max are indicated, you need to hit max to consistently turn it on
